# The wasm cascade isolates a dead rotor — read through the published seam

**Date:** 2026-10-01 · **Rig:** gz sim 8.15.0, `worlds/falcon-quad.sdf`, macOS arm64
**Harness:** `tests/cascade-sitl-wasm` (`BACKEND=gazebo`, `--features gazebo`)
**Cited by:** FV-FALCON-FAULT-005 (SWREQ-FALCON-FAULT-P02)

## What was missing

FV-FALCON-FAULT-005's own *EVIDENCE REQUIRED* clause asks for the rotor-out
scenario on the gz plant **"native AND wasm"**, three pristine-server trials
each. It was promoted to `verified` on 2026-09-25 with the **native half only**
— `--scenario=supervised-rotorout` drives the native `FlightSupervisor`, which
the artifact says outright. This file measures the wasm half.

The leg was not merely unmeasured, it was **unjudgeable**. Its `peak_tilt` was
`sqrt(ax² + ay²)` — lateral specific force, not tilt — and there was no way to
ask the component *which* rotor it had isolated. The `@0.11.0` observer export
(TRANSPORT-P01) is what makes the verdict possible: `failed-rotor` is read
through the published Component Model ABI, not inferred from the trajectory.

## Measurement — rotor 0 killed at 10.0 s of 25 s, 250 Hz

Three trials, each against a **pristine server** (the gz rig rule — the world
does not reset; `scripts/gz-trial.rs` forks a fresh one per trial).

```sh
./scripts/gz-trial.rs --label G1 --env BACKEND=gazebo --env FAIL_ROTOR=0@10 -- \
    ./tests/cascade-sitl-wasm/target/release/cascade-sitl-wasm \
    wasm/cm/cascade/target/wasm32-unknown-unknown/release/falcon_cascade_cm.wasm 6250 0.004
```

| trial | isolated | latency | touchdown after loss | peak true tilt | peak horiz | final NED |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | `Some(0)` | 2 ticks (10 ms) | 1.43 s | 0.184 rad | 0.94 m | n−0.890 e0.300 |
| G2 | `Some(0)` | 2 ticks (10 ms) | 1.42 s | 0.196 rad | 1.01 m | n−0.964 e0.313 |
| G3 | `Some(0)` | 2 ticks (10 ms) | 2.00 s | **0.381 rad** | 1.01 m | n−0.697 e0.119 |

**PASS 3/3.** The seqlock held alongside it: 6250 read-state calls, coherent
every tick, valid mask `0x7f`.

### Against the native leg, same plant, same scenario shape

| | native (V1–V3) | wasm (G1–G3) |
|---|---|---|
| isolated | `Some(0)` 3/3 | `Some(0)` 3/3 |
| peak tilt after | 0.197 – 0.213 rad | 0.184 – 0.381 rad |
| peak horiz after | 0.95 – 1.00 m | 0.94 – 1.01 m |
| flight ends | disarm at 1.26 s | touchdown at 1.42–2.00 s |

The horizontal excursion matches to within 0.06 m. **The tilt does not:** G3's
0.381 rad is 76% of the 0.5 rad bar — a 1.3× margin where the native leg has
2.3×. The wasm leg's trial-to-trial tilt spread (0.184→0.381) is also wider
than native's (0.197→0.213). Recorded as the weakest part of this result, not
smoothed over. The component receives none of the bench's gz tuning, which is
the obvious candidate and is **not** established here.

## It can fail — negative control on the same plant

`NO_RPM=1` withholds ESC telemetry, leaving the FDI inert. Same binary, same
world, same kill:

| | ESC telem | isolated | peak true tilt | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FDI live | 6250/6250 | `Some(0)` | 0.184–0.381 rad | PASS |
| `NO_RPM=1` | **0/6250** | **`None`** | **3.142 rad (180°)** | **FAIL** |

The airframe inverts completely and never touches down. Two independent bars
fire (isolation, then tilt), so a FAIL here is caused by a defect and not by a
harness artifact — and it reproduces, deliberately, the 180° inversion this
property first failed with (FAULT-005's `rot1`).

## Three harness defects found getting here

Each one made a number wrong, and all three are the same shape: something
measured nothing while reporting cleanly.

**1. The harness had no floor.** The native rotor-out scenario calls
`set_ground_contact(true)` — *"This scenario LANDS, so the plant needs a floor
to land on"* (`examples/falcon-sitl-gz/src/main.rs:1484`). This harness never
did. On the mock plant the airframe sank **1.01 m below the ground plane** and
kept flying. That is the defect the mock plant's own ground comment already
records: *"a rotor-out scenario ended 359 m below its launch point while the
verdict called it landed."* Now enabled, **gated on `FAIL_ROTOR`** to match
native — the clamp feeds the specific-force derivation (#485), so enabling it
unconditionally would move the nominal hold this harness is the CI oracle for.
Re-verified unchanged at 1.96 m.

**2. The judged window now ends at touchdown.** Nothing terminates this
flight: the component wraps `FlightCore`, so there is no failsafe to disarm
(#414). `peak_horiz` over a whole run is therefore a function of the tick
budget, not of the control law — it measured **24.91 m** on mock, of which
**21.65 m accumulated after the vehicle had already reached the ground**. The
floor clamps only `p_ned[2]` and `v_ned[2]`, so a still-powered airframe slides
on a frictionless plane at a constant 0.029 rad tilt:

```
t=15.02  touchdown           horiz 3.26 m
t=16.00  down 0.00  v=(0.23,1.41,0.00)   horiz  4.60 m
t=20.00  down 0.00  v=(0.30,2.06,0.00)   horiz 11.67 m
t=24.00  down 0.00  v=(0.37,2.58,0.00)   horiz 21.10 m
```

Touchdown is **defined by the physics, not chosen** — and it is what the native
leg measured (loss 10.000 s → landing 11.264 s). The rule and its reason were
written into the harness before the number it produces was looked at. The
full-run figure is still printed and labelled, as the measure of the missing
failsafe. An **empty window now FAILS** rather than meeting every bar vacuously
at 0.0.

**3. `gz-trial.rs`'s `KEEP` allowlist swallowed the entire verdict.**
`line.contains(k)` is case-sensitive, so the existing lowercase `"rotor"` does
not match `"ROTOR-OUT"`. This is the **third** trace this allowlist has
silently eaten, after `FDI_TRACE` and `RING_TRACE`. A dropped verdict reads as
a quiet pass.

## Not claimed

- **The real airframe.** This is the gz plant.
- **That wasm and native are equivalent.** The horizontal excursions match
  closely; the tilt margins do not (1.3× vs 2.3×). Three trials per side is
  too few to call the tilt spread either way.
- **Any tuning explanation for that spread.** The component receives none of
  the bench's gz calibration beyond the `configure` seam's values. Untested.
- **A failsafe.** There is none in this component (#414), which is exactly why
  the judged window has to be bounded by the physics.
- **The mock plant's numbers as comparable.** Mock descends far more slowly
  (touchdown 5.02 s after the loss, vs 1.42–2.00 s on gz) and its judged
  excursion is 3.28 m. Different plant, different number; the gz rows above are
  the evidence.

## Reproduce

```sh
cargo build --release -p falcon-sitl-gz --features gazebo
cd tests/cascade-sitl-wasm && cargo build --release --features gazebo && cd -
for L in G1 G2 G3; do
  ./scripts/gz-trial.rs --label $L --env BACKEND=gazebo --env FAIL_ROTOR=0@10 -- \
    ./tests/cascade-sitl-wasm/target/release/cascade-sitl-wasm \
    wasm/cm/cascade/target/wasm32-unknown-unknown/release/falcon_cascade_cm.wasm 6250 0.004
done
# negative control — must FAIL on isolation:
./scripts/gz-trial.rs --label GNEG --env BACKEND=gazebo --env FAIL_ROTOR=0@10 --env NO_RPM=1 -- ...
# ROTOR_TRACE=1 adds the post-fault shape (t, horiz, down, velocity, tilt) every 250 ticks.
```
