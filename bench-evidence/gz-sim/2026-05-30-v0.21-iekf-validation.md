# v0.21 — Invariant EKF validation (Mahony vs IEKF in gz)

Date: 2026-05-30
Backend: Gazebo Harmonic, `--home=47.3977,8.5456,488`, `hover` scenario.
Branch: `falcon-v0.21-iekf` (WIP — NOT released; does not yet fly).

## The categorical result

The IEKF (relay-iekf) runs in the gz cascade fed IMU (propagate) + gz
NavSat position (update_position). Body is physically level
(`true_tilt` < 2° early). Attitude-estimate tilt, the quantity v0.20
showed the Mahony filter getting catastrophically wrong:

```
 t     true   Mahony    IEKF
 5.0   0.1°    3.3°      2.8°
 6.0   0.5°   25.7°      8.8°
 6.5   0.5°   48.7°     10.0°
 7.0   1.0°   95.8°      9.0°
 7.5   2.0°  140.8°     11.3°   (parallel run, control on Mahony)
```

The Mahony filter flips to **140°** (complete loss); the IEKF tracks
within **~10°**. Its position estimate tracks gz truth closely
(`ipos ≈ true pos` throughout). **140° → 10° is the keystone working.**

## Closing the loop on the IEKF

Switching the cascade's control attitude source to the IEKF (`USE_IEKF`):

- final_dist = **34.7 m** (vs the Mahony cascade's **206 m**) over 30 s.
- Trajectory: the IEKF tracks *excellently* at hover (t=0–2.5 s: IEKF
  tilt 0.0–0.7° vs true 0.1°; body climbs to −2 m and holds, pos ≈ 0),
  then a small attitude error (~3° at t=3 s) feeds the position
  controller → the body tilts → translates → attitude becomes less
  observable → error grows → drift.

## Honest status

- The IEKF **architecture is validated**: at hover it is near-perfect
  (0.1°) where Mahony runs away; its position estimate is excellent; and
  closing the loop on it improves 206 m → 34 m.
- It does **NOT yet close position-hold**. The residual is a coupled
  estimator-controller instability: with IMU + GPS only, *attitude
  during translation* is weakly observable, so a few-degree error
  initiates a self-reinforcing drift.
