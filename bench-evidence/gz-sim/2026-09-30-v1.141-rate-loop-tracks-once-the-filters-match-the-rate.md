# The rate loop tracks once the filters are designed for the rate they run at

**Date:** 2026-09-30 · **Rig:** gz sim 8.15.0, `worlds/falcon-quad.sdf`, macOS arm64
**Cited by:** FV-FALCON-RATE-002 (SWREQ-FALCON-RATE-P05), FV-FALCON-FAULT-005

## The one-line cause

`gyro_lpf` and the harmonic `notch` are filter **designs** — their coefficients
come from a sample rate. `CascadePartition::new` took that rate from whatever the
**caller declared**:

```
FlightSupervisor::new  ->  FlightCore::new(0.5, 1000.0)    hardcoded
gz bench               ->  stepped at 250 Hz
run_flightcore         ->  FlightCore::new(hover, 1.0/dt)  correct
```

A 60 Hz low-pass designed for 1 kHz and fed at 250 Hz has its real corner at a
quarter of the intended frequency. That single difference is why one path rang
and the other didn't, on the same plant and the same commit.

**It was never the gz plant.** #270 attributed it to the plant from
2026-07-14 until now.

## Measurement — unfaulted hover, `t ∈ [5,10) s`, 1250 ticks per trial

Three trials, each against a **pristine server** (the world does not reset).
`--backend=gazebo --scenario=supervised-rotorout --duration=25`.

`rp_rate2` and `gate_open` are read from `FlightCore::fdi_diag()` — the
**low-pass filtered** gyro the gate actually tests, not the raw one.

| trial | roll/pitch RMS | motor min | motor max | gate open |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | 0.00174 rad/s | 0.5813 | 0.5860 | **100.0%** |
| V2 | 0.00174 rad/s | 0.5813 | 0.5860 | **100.0%** |
| V3 | 0.00180 rad/s | 0.5810 | 0.5863 | **100.0%** |

RATE-P05's bars are RMS < 0.1 rad/s, no motor within 2% of a limit, gate open
≥ 95%. **PASS 3/3** — a 57× RMS margin, motors in a 0.005-wide band, and the
gate never closes once.

## Before / after, same window

| | declared 1000 Hz, stepped 250 | matched |
|---|---|---|
| roll/pitch RMS | 2.47 rad/s | **0.0017 rad/s** |
| motors | 0.12 ↔ 1.0000 | 0.5810 – 0.5863 |
| gate open | ~66% | **100.0%** |

## Rotor-out, the downstream property

Same fix, `#474` verdict, three pristine trials:

| trial | isolated | peak tilt after | peak horiz after | landed / disarmed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | Some(0) | 0.197 rad | 0.97 m | 11.264 s / yes |
| V2 | Some(0) | 0.213 rad | 1.00 m | 11.264 s / yes |
| V3 | Some(0) | 0.200 rad | 0.95 m | 11.264 s / yes |

**PASS 3/3**, against **FAIL 11/12** with three 180° inversions before. Peak
tilt 0.197–0.213 rad against the 0.5 bar is a 2.3× margin.

Control, same rig: `--scenario=flightcore` PASSES at `final_dist 0.12 m`,
`horizontal 0.01 m` — unchanged by the fix, so it costs the hold nothing.

## It can fail

Reverting `resync_loop_rate` restores 2.47 rad/s RMS and a gate shut a third of
the time. The bars were not chosen to be met: the same scenario under the same
verdict measured 11 FAIL / 1 PASS before the fix.

## Not claimed

- **The real airframe.** This is the gz plant.
- **The wasm cascade.** `supervised-rotorout` drives the native
  `FlightSupervisor`, and the component receives none of the bench's tuning.
- **That the anti-windup fixes were the cause.** Both were real defects and are
  fixed, but they were treating symptoms — the ESO was never the problem, the
  filter in front of it was. Each moved the number a little and neither cured it.

## A measurement error worth keeping on the record

An earlier round computed the gate-shut fraction from the **raw** gyro and
reported 96.8% where the filtered figure was 33.8%. The clean-room reviewer
caught it by arithmetic before any re-run: a gate shut 96.8% of the time that
never reopens cannot produce 9 isolations in 12 trials. `RING_TRACE` now emits
the gate's own filtered values so that class of error cannot recur — and
`gz-trial.rs`'s `KEEP` allowlist gained `"RING "`, after it had silently
swallowed the whole trace exactly as it previously did for `FDI_TRACE`.

## Reproduce

```sh
export RING_TRACE=1
./scripts/gz-trial.rs --label V1 -- ./target/release/falcon-sitl-gz \
    --backend=gazebo --scenario=supervised-rotorout --duration=25
# columns: RING t gx gy gz wdx wdy wdz tqx tqy tqz m0 m1 m2 m3 rp_rate2_f tilt_cos gate_open
# the last three are the gate's OWN quantities; gx/gy are RAW and must not be
# used for a gate-shut fraction.
```
