# v0.23 — geometric SE(3) attitude control (relay-geo)

Date: 2026-05-30
Branch: `falcon-v0.21-iekf` (WIP). gz Harmonic, `--home=47.3977,8.5456,488`.

## What shipped (unit-tested, 5 tests)

`relay-geo` — geometric SE(3) attitude controller (Lee/Leok/McClamroch),
the fix for the v0.22 finding that the Euler PID cascade couples tilt
into yaw once heading ≠ 0. Works directly on SO(3):
- `desired_attitude(b3_d, yaw)` — R_d from thrust direction + heading,
  geometrically (no Euler, no singularity, no coupling).
- `attitude_error` — e_R = ½(R_dᵀR − RᵀR_d)^∨.
- `moment` — M = −k_R e_R − k_Ω Ω + Ω × JΩ.
- `psi` — the Lyapunov potential ½tr(I − R_dᵀR).

Proven in unit tests:
- **`attitude_error_does_not_cross_couple`**: a tilt at heading=90°
  produces ZERO yaw error (the exact Euler bug, absent); pure yaw error
  → zero roll/pitch.
- **`geometric_control_drives_psi_to_zero`**: simulated rigid body from
  40° tilt — Ψ strictly decreases to ~0 (almost-global exp. stability,
  in-crate evidence for the future Lean proof).

## gz closed-loop (geo-hover scenario): IEKF + geometric + thrust-floor

The geometric controller **fixes the tilt coupling** — where the Euler
cascade tipped the body to 46° and crashed, the geometric controller
keeps it **perfectly level** (tilt 0.0–0.2°) throughout. Altitude holds.

**ATT_ONLY (level hold, no horizontal command): PASS — final 0.29 m,
rms_steady 0.32 m / 20 s** (the ±0.5 m / 1.0 m bar). The verified stack —
IEKF (full state) + geometric SE(3) attitude + thrust-floor mixer +
arming sequencer — holds position at level.

## The remaining gap — a maneuver-induced yaw spin

With active horizontal control, the body yaws and the position drifts.
Systematically localized:
- **Not the estimator's state**: IEKF tilt + position excellent; compass
  correct (Pose_V tag 2); per-axis q_gyro keeps yaw observable.
- **Not a control sign / gain**: tilt control perfect; boosting yaw gain
  5× does not stop the spin.
- **Not an SDF imbalance**: under open-loop equal motors the true yaw
  stays exactly 0 — the airframe is balanced.
- **It is a coupled allocation + estimation-rate issue**: the
  controller's roll/pitch corrections create motor-drag yaw disturbances
  (the static mixer doesn't model the drag-vs-thrust coupling), and the
  IEKF yaw *lags* the resulting fast spin (the compass update can't keep
  pace), so the controller chases a stale yaw → persistent spin. A level
  spinning body doesn't translate (hence ATT_ONLY holds), but horizontal
  maneuvering during the spin drifts.

This points squarely at the next roadmap steps:
- **v0.24 control allocation** — a mixer that models the rotor drag
  torque (thrust ≈ kω², drag ≈ bω²) so roll/pitch commands don't leak
  into uncommanded yaw; the PX4-style sequential-desaturation allocator
  with the real effectiveness matrix (per the v0.24 research).
- **v0.25 INDI** — sensor-based incremental control is inherently robust
  to exactly this kind of unmodeled actuator coupling.
