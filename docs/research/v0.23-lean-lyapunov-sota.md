# v0.23 — Formally Proving Geometric SE(3) Controller Stability (research note)

Scope: the geometric SE(3) attitude/position controller (Lee et al. 2010)

## 1. The classical result to formalize (Lee, Leok, McClamroch, CDC 2010)

On SO(3): configuration-error function **Ψ(R,R_d) = ½ tr(I − R_dᵀR)**,
positive-definite on **L₂ = {R : Ψ < 2}** (angle < 180°); attitude error
**e_R = ½(R_dᵀR − RᵀR_d)^∨**; angular-velocity error
**e_Ω = Ω − RᵀR_d Ω_d**. Under the moment law
`M = −k_R e_R − k_Ω e_Ω + Ω×JΩ − J(Ω̂ RᵀR_d Ω_d − RᵀR_d Ω̇_d)`,
**Proposition 1** gives EXPONENTIAL stability of (e_R,e_Ω)=(0,0) provided
**Ψ(0) < 2 and ‖e_Ω(0)‖² < 2k_R(2−Ψ(0))/λ_M(J)**. The proof uses a
Lyapunov function with a CROSS-TERM `V = ½ e_Ω·J e_Ω + k_R Ψ + c⟨e_R,e_Ω⟩`
(the c-coupling buys exponential, not just asymptotic, decay) and reduces
to **V̇ ≤ −zᵀM₂z** negative-definite, z=(‖e_R‖,‖e_Ω‖), a 2×2 matrix
inequality. Ψ<2 ⇒ almost-global (excluded set is measure-zero).
Sources: [Lee 2010 PDF](https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~mleok/pdf/LeLeMc2010_quadrotor.pdf),
[arXiv:1003.2005](https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.2005).

**Discharge target**: the ALGEBRAIC inequality — V positive-definite and
V̇ negative-definite (the 2×2 form) on Ψ<2, GIVEN the closed-loop vector
field. Finite polynomial-in-(e_R,e_Ω) + trace identities; not inherently
transcendental.

## 2. SO(3)/Lie-group control in proof assistants (2024–2026)

- **Lean/Mathlib**: `Matrix`, `Matrix.trace`, `specialOrthogonalGroup`,
  `fderiv`, inner-product/bilinear infra. The hat/vee maps and ½tr(I−R_dᵀR)
  identities are expressible + provable today (linear-algebra/trace facts).
- **Rocq**: MathComp + Coquelicot; SO(3)/hat map have prior formalization.
- **Realistic split**: the ALGEBRAIC layer (trace identities, ‖e_R‖²
  bounds, the 2×2 negative-definiteness) is fully formalizable now. The
  DYNAMIC layer (flow ⇒ convergence, LaSalle) is the gap.

## 3. Lyapunov/stability in Lean — Mathlib status (decisive)

As of **Feb 2026**, Mathlib has ODE existence/uniqueness
(`Analysis.ODE.PicardLindelof`) and now flows/Grönwall
([arXiv:2602.13247](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.13247)), but **NO Lyapunov
stability, NO asymptotic stability, NO LaSalle** — and those authors say
it is not even in announced future work. So a full Lean "trajectory ⇒
converges" proof would require formalizing Lyapunov/LaSalle in Mathlib
first — a multi-year effort, NOT a v0.23 deliverable.

**Credible Lean scope = the algebraic core**: (a) V positive-definite on
Ψ<2; (b) V̇ = ∇V·f_cl negative-definite (the 2×2 inequality) at the
vector-field level; cite the trajectory-integration step as a stated lemma
(Lee 2010 Prop. 1 + the forthcoming Mathlib flow API).

## 3b. Alternative discharge tactics

- **SOS + exact arithmetic** (Magron/Roux,
  [JAR 2024](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10817-024-09717-2)):
  verified SOS Lyapunov certificates — handles the positivity obligation
  but needs a POLYNOMIAL field (SO(3) is non-polynomial w/o reparam).
- **dReal / δ-decidability** (Gao–Avigad–Clarke,
  [arXiv:1204.3513](https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.3513)): δ-complete SMT over
  reals WITH trig/transcendentals — natively handles the sin/cos rotation
  entries, the standard Lyapunov-condition backend. Least formalization
  surface; caveat is δ-soundness (explicit numerical margin), not exact.
- **Verus/Z3**: only a polynomial relaxation; nlsat incomplete on transcendentals.

Assessor view (DO-178C/EN 50128): a Lean kernel-checked proof of the
algebraic core = highest assurance for that obligation; dReal = credible
reproducible certificate for the full nonlinear inequality with a stated δ.
Neither alone closes the trajectory-integration step → cite the literature.
