# Background research — v0.23 control layer SOTA (May 2026)

Scouted while building v0.21 (per the "research the next version" directive).
Question: is there something MORE provable than Lee SE(3) geometric control
for the v0.23 attitude+position controller?

## Recommendation (confirmed + refined)

**Core: Lee/Leok/McClamroch SE(3) geometric tracking control
(arXiv:1003.2005), extended with geometric integral action
(Goodarzi/Lee, arXiv:1304.6765).** It is the *only* option with a
closed-form, hand-derivable Lyapunov certificate over the full coupled
6-DOF state, almost-global exponential stability, and NO solver/learned
artifact in the certificate — by far the most realistic to discharge in
Lean/Rocq + SMT. Integral action preserves the proof structure and buys
parametric/disturbance robustness.

**Honest claim: "almost-global", never "global."** The measure-zero
excluded set is the true SO(3) topological obstruction (no continuous
controller can be globally stabilising), not a proof gap. Stating it
precisely is itself part of the rigor.

## The alternatives, and why not for v0.23

- **Neural-Lyapunov certified control** (arXiv:2404.07956, 2411.18235):
  verified results top out at the **2D (planar, ~6-state) quadrotor**;
  NO published full-3D/12-state verified neural controller. The artifact
  is a tool-specific (α,β-CROWN) bound-prop certificate, not a
  human-readable proof — *less* aligned with a Lean/Rocq story, not more.
  Track as a future "learned+certified" line; not now.
- **Contraction / control contraction metrics** (arXiv:2109.04453):
  intrinsic, coordinate-free, SDP→SOS-checkable — philosophically the
  best *future* alternative to Lee — but no global SE(3) result yet
  (model isn't control-affine in a global chart). Research track.
- **INDI** (planned v0.25 inner loop): provably stable only **locally**,
  via singular-perturbation theory, for large inner-loop gain + bounded
  actuator bandwidth (Wang et al., 10.2514/1.G003791). Real but
  hard-to-mechanize proof (two-time-scale). Use INDI under the SE(3)
  outer law; be honest the time-scale-separation step is the weakest
  formal link; do NOT claim global stability.

## Reserve, for the v0.28 shield

**Compatible CLF-CBF with SOS-verified certificates (arXiv:2406.18914)**
— necessary-and-sufficient compatibility conditions verified via
Sum-of-Squares, demonstrated on a 3D quadrotor, code at
github.com/hongkai-dai/compatible_clf_cbf. SOS witnesses are exactly
re-checkable (Positivstellensatz-in-Coq workflow). NOT a tracking
controller — a safety filter layered on the SE(3) core. The cleanest
"provable safety AND stability" composition for the verified shield.

## Roadmap impact

- v0.23: Lee SE(3) **+ integral action** (was just "SE(3)"). Lean proof
  target = the explicit closed-form Lyapunov; hardest part is the SO(3)
  error-function lemmas (Ψ(R,R_d) bounds, trace/skew identities).
- v0.28 shield: compatible CLF-CBF (SOS), not a bespoke construction.
- New research tracks logged: CCM (future SE(3) alternative),
  neural-Lyapunov (when it reaches 3D).
