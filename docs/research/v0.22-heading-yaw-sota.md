# v0.22 — Heading / Yaw Estimation SOTA (research note)

Scope: add magnetometer + magless-yaw recovery to the v0.21 Invariant EKF
on SE₂(3) (15-state error covariance `[δθ, δv, δp, δb_g, δb_a]`, already
doing position, compass-yaw, and gravity-tilt updates). Removes the
remaining **gz-truth heading cheat** (the bench currently reads heading
from the ground-truth `PoseV` orientation — analogous to how v0.21's IEKF
removed v0.20's gz-truth *position* crutch).

## 1. Magnetometer as an invariant measurement

In the Barrau–Bonnabel framework, IMU navigation on SE₂(3) is
*group-affine* → state-independent invariant-error dynamics (the source of
the IEKF's convergence guarantees, [arXiv:1410.1465](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1410.1465)).
A measurement keeps that structure only if it is itself invariant. A
magnetometer reading `y = Rᵀ·m_world` (body-frame read of a known world
reference vector) is **right-invariant — the SAME algebraic form as the
gravity-tilt update already implemented**. So it reuses the existing
right-invariant measurement path; the innovation lives in body frame and
the H-Jacobian touches δθ.

**Pitfalls (the real work):**
- **Couples roll/pitch.** A raw 3-axis mag update double-counts against
  the gravity-tilt update and corrupts well-observed tilt with magnetic
  disturbance. Fix: **project to heading-only** (tilt-compensate using the
  filter's roll/pitch → scalar yaw innovation). This is what PX4 does.
- **Declination/inclination.** `m_world` needs a world magnetic model:
  declination rotates magnetic→true north; inclination's vertical
  component must NOT leak into yaw.
- **Hard-iron (additive bias) / soft-iron (ellipsoidal scale/skew).**
  Calibrate by ellipsoid fit (offline/static params for verifiability;
  online estimation later). [NXP AN4246](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/application-note/AN4246.pdf).

## 2. PX4 EKF2 EKF-GSF yaw estimator

A bank of **N small 3-state EKFs** (vN, vE, yaw) seeded across a grid of
yaw hypotheses, driven by IMU + GPS velocity, combined by a **Gaussian Sum
Filter** into `yaw_composite` + variance. Needs no prior yaw and no mag;
triggers an emergency yaw reset of the main filter after a post-takeoff
nav failure. **Strength:** recovers a globally-ambiguous quantity by
covering the circle. **Limit:** needs horizontal acceleration to become
observable; heuristic, hand-tuned, essentially **unverifiable**.

## 3. Yaw observability without a magnetometer

Yaw about gravity is **unobservable from IMU+position alone**
([arXiv:2507.02198](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.02198)). It becomes
observable through: course-over-ground (weak — quads fly sideways);
dual-antenna GPS (extra HW); or **persistence of excitation of inertial
specific force** — constant velocity ⇒ unobservable, genuine horizontal
acceleration ⇒ observable, stated as a theorem in
[van Goor–Hamel–Mahony, arXiv:2308.11124](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.11124).
This is exactly what GSF exploits, but as a *checkable condition*.

## 4. Newer / more provable than GSF (2024–2026)

- **Equivariant Filter (EqF)** ([arXiv:2010.14666](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.14666),
  TAC 2023): generalizes the IEKF to homogeneous spaces; a strict superset
  and the natural upgrade path.
- **Constructive equivariant INS observer** ([arXiv:2308.11124](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.11124)):
  **almost-global asymptotic + local exponential stability with GNSS
  position only, gains-independent**, conditioned on acceleration PE — the
  *provable* magless-yaw result (Lyapunov, Lean/Rocq-amenable).
- **Geometric Gaussian fusion on Lie groups** ([arXiv:2403.16411](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.16411),
  2024): rigorous merge of concentrated Gaussians at different group points
  — the right way to fuse hypotheses on SO(3) if a bank is ever needed.

## 5. Recommendation for v0.22 (ranked)

1. **Heading-only magnetometer update on the existing right-invariant
   SE₂(3) path (primary deliverable).** Lowest risk, reuses the
   gravity-tilt/yaw update structure, fully verifiable: 1-D innovation,
   offline ellipsoid calibration + WMM declination as parameters.
2. **A provable acceleration-PE yaw observer as the magless fallback —
   NOT GSF.** Encode the observability condition as an explicit gate
   ("yaw aiding trusted only when ∫‖a_horiz‖ over a window exceeds a
   threshold") — itself a checkable predicate with a Lyapunov backing.
   Single observer, no_std/no_alloc, no filter bank.
3. **Defer a full GSF bank.** If true multi-hypothesis recovery (180° at
   rest) is later required, use a small fixed bank fused via the Lie-group
   Gaussian-fusion method — only after 1 and 2.

**Bottom line:** ship the invariant heading-only mag update now; for
magless yaw prefer the provable acceleration-excitation observer over
PX4's GSF — it matches the IEKF/equivariant foundation and the provability
mandate, whereas GSF buys robustness at the cost of being unverifiable.
